# Doctor Visit Log

**AppADay 175** | Health | [Live app](https://augustineiacopelli.github.io/appaday-175-doctor-visit-log/) | [Portfolio](https://augustineiacopelli.github.io/appaday/)

Prepare for, record, and follow up on every doctor visit for the whole family, whether it is the family doctor, a specialist, urgent care, telehealth, or a procedure. Everything stays on your device.

## What it does

Each visit has four tabs.

**Prep.** Set the date, time, provider, and visit type, then write the main reason in one sentence and what you want to walk out with. Log each symptom or concern the way doctors ask about it: where, when it started, how bad on a 0 to 10 slider, the pattern, and what makes it worse or better. Write questions and star your top three so they get asked first, or tap suggested questions matched to the visit type (Ask Me 3, diagnosis, tests, medicines, treatment, specialist, screenings, procedure, and next steps). A bring list adapts to the visit type, so a first specialist visit reminds you about the referral, outside records, and prior authorization. Bring forward pulls unanswered questions and symptoms from the last visit, preferring the same provider.

**Visit.** Record who you actually saw, vitals, what the doctor found, the diagnosis, the Ask Me 3 answers, the plan, and the warning signs that mean call right away. Check off your questions with their answers. Log medicine changes (start, change dose, stop, keep taking) and push them to the person's med list in one tap. Track tests from ordered to results in, and referrals from needs scheduling to seen.

**After.** To dos with due dates and quick add chips, the next appointment, a Plan the follow up button that creates the next visit with the same provider and carries over unanswered questions, billing notes, and general notes.

**Summary.** A prep sheet and a visit recap, each printable on one page, copyable as plain text, and shareable. The prep sheet includes the person's medicines, allergies, conditions, surgeries, and family history, with blank note boxes for the visit itself.

The Home tab shows the next visit with a countdown and prep progress, visits still waiting for a recap, open to dos across every visit (overdue in red), results you are still waiting on, and referrals to book. Visits can be searched across symptoms, diagnoses, notes, medicines, and tests, and filtered by upcoming, past, primary care, specialists, or open items. The Doctors tab keeps each provider's specialty, practice, phone, fax, portal, and address, with call, portal, map, and history buttons. The Health tab holds each person's profile and medicine list, lists their diagnoses on record, and prints a health summary with the care team. Upcoming visits export to your calendar as an .ics file with a reminder the day before.

## Family and privacy

Add family members in Settings to keep a separate log and profile for each person you take to the doctor. There is no account and no server. Data lives in this browser's localStorage, so download a backup file from Settings now and then; restoring from it replaces what is on the device. This app helps you organize and does not give medical advice.

## Built with

A single index.html of vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Google Fonts (DM Serif Display and Inter). No Claude API. Home screen icon, web manifest, and save helper come from the AppADay portal.

Question prompts draw on the AHRQ patient tips and the Ask Me 3 program; symptom fields follow the questions clinicians use to take a symptom history.
